## Implementation Notes

- Evidence is in `docs/designs/2026-09-02-severity-scale.md`: across eighteen months of the
  incident log, "degraded" and "partial outage" were used interchangeably by the same
  people for incidents of the same shape.
- `monitoring` replaces it and answers a question customers actually ask.
- The error lists all four allowed values, because someone hitting it is mid-incident and
  should not have to open the source to find out what is legal.
