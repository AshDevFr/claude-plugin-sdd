## Implementation Notes

- **Blocked on purpose, and not being worked around.** Four candidates are on the mockup at
  `docs/designs/mockups/2026-09-12-status-page/severity.html`. Writing a palette now means
  writing it twice, and the reason for asking was to avoid exactly that.
- Colour never carries the meaning alone: every severity is also a word on the page, so a
  reader with any colour vision deficiency loses nothing. The palette still has to pass
  contrast, which is the second half of this task.
- The current colours are placeholders from phase 1 and should not be mistaken for a
  decision.
